Privacy Policy

MU Piping Calculator
Last updated: 29 July 2026

Summary: MU Piping Calculator is an engineering utility app. We do not require an account, we do not sell personal data, and calculations run on your device.

1. Who we are

MU Piping Calculator (“the App”) provides piping engineering calculators and reference data for educational and professional guidance (Oil & Gas, petrochemical, offshore, refinery, construction).

2. Information we collect

The App is designed to work with minimal data collection:

  • On-device settings only: theme (dark/light), language preference, favorites, and recent calculator list may be stored locally on your device (e.g. browser localStorage or app storage).
  • No account: We do not require sign-up, email, or phone number to use the calculators.
  • No calculation upload: Inputs and results are processed on your device and are not sent to our servers for storage.

3. Hosting & technical data

If you use the web version, the site may be hosted on a third-party static host (for example GitHub Pages). That host may automatically log standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, and date/time of access as part of normal web server operation. We do not use this for advertising profiles.

If you install the Android app package (TWA / PWA wrapper), Google Play and Android may process install/diagnostics data under Google’s policies.

4. Permissions

The App does not require access to contacts, camera, microphone, location, or files for core calculator features. Optional system features (share sheet, print/PDF) use the device’s built-in tools and stay under your control.

5. Cookies & analytics

We do not use third-party advertising cookies. Local storage is used only for preferences and favorites described above. If analytics are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before they are enabled.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for adult engineering and technical users. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country).

7. Engineering disclaimer

Results are for guidance only. Always verify critical design values against applicable codes (e.g. ASME), project specifications, and a qualified engineer. We are not liable for decisions made solely on App output.

8. Data retention

Local preferences remain on your device until you clear site/app data or uninstall the App. We do not operate a personal-data database for App users.

9. Your choices

  • Clear browser site data or app storage to remove favorites/recent/theme/language.
  • Uninstall the App at any time.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

For privacy questions about MU Piping Calculator, contact the developer through the support email or contact channel listed on the Google Play store listing (or the GitHub repository where the App is published).
